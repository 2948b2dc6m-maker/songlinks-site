Quick FAQ

What is SongLinks?

A music party game for iPhone. A song plays. On your turn, name a real song title and artist that shares a word with it. Keep the chain going.

How many players?

1–6 players around one iPhone, or a solo game.

How do we play?

  1. Set the iPhone on the table where everyone can see and hear it.
  2. A song plays. Its title and artist give you the words to link from.
  3. On your turn, tap the mic and say a song title and artist that shares one of those words.
  4. Get it right and everyone hears your song. Miss and it’s a strike.

Why does SongLinks need the microphone and speech recognition?

So you can say your guess out loud. The mic is only on after you tap it on your turn. SongLinks doesn’t record or save your voice.

I tapped “Don’t Allow.” How do I turn the mic back on?

Go to your iPhone’s Settings > SongLinks > Microphone and turn it on. Turn on Speech Recognition there too.

Do I need a Bluetooth speaker?

It’s optional but recommended to fill the room with music. Keep the iPhone on the table, because answers still go through the phone’s mic.

Can I make the text bigger?

Yes. SongLinks supports larger text sizes (iPhone Settings > Display & Brightness > Text Size).

Does it work on iPad?

SongLinks is made for iPhone.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. Songs and previews come from the iTunes Store over the internet.

Do I need an account?

No. Just open it and play. If you’re signed in to Game Center, you can also see the SongLinks leaderboards: High Scores, Daily Challenge, and Daily Challenge: All-Time. Game Center data is handled by Apple under its terms.

It didn’t understand my guess.

Speak clearly and say both the title and the artist. A quieter room helps.
